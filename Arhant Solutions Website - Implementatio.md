Arhant Solutions Website - Implementation Plan
Our objective is to design a high-end, premium B2B SaaS landing page for Arhant Solutions. As your UI designer, I will ensure a minimal, clean user interface with dynamic micro-interactions to emphasize the brand's modernity and capability in the insurance software space.

Project Architecture & Tech Stack
Framework: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript
Styling: Tailwind CSS
Typography: Brawler (Headings), Rubik (Body text)
Palette: Primary #ff6400, Secondary #000000, Accent #FFFFFF
Animations: Framer Motion (for scroll and hover animations, providing fluid, professional transitions)
Development Phases (Section by Section)
To ensure the highest quality, we will develop this site step-by-step, reviewing the design and functionality of each section together:

Phase 1: Project Setup & Global Theming
Initializing Next.js app, installing dependencies (Tailwind, Framer Motion), and setting up global fonts and colors.
Phase 2: Hero Layout & Navigation
The first impression. A modern navbar and a stunning hero area to communicate "Best Insurance Software provider in Nepal."
Phase 3: About Us, Mission & Vision
A clean layout presenting the company's objective and core values with subtle scroll unveil animations.
Phase 4: Our Solutions Overview
An interactive grid or carousel showcasing Life, General, Aggregator, and Micro Insurance.
Phase 5: Detailed Products (IEnsure Suite)
Highlighting IEnsure Life, General, and Micro with their major features utilizing modern glassmorphism or sleek card designs.
Phase 6: Technology Partners
A smooth infinite scroll or animated logo ticker.
Phase 7: Blog, FAQ, & Footer
Accordions for FAQ, aesthetic blog cards, and the Contact Us footprint.
